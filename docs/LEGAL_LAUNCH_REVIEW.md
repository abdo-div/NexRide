# Legal launch review

The customer-facing Terms, Privacy Notice, and Cancellation and Refund Policy
are implemented in English and Arabic. Before production launch, company
management and qualified Libyan counsel must approve the published wording and
record that approval.

Confirm the following against the final operating company and contracts:

- the operator's exact registered legal name, commercial registration number,
  physical notice address, support email, and telephone number;
- the rental companies' standard rental agreement, insurance, deposit, driver
  eligibility, damage, traffic-fine, and permitted-use terms;
- the production payment provider agreement and the operational process that
  executes refunds recorded by NexRide;
- accounting, booking, identity, partner-document, security-log, and newsletter
  retention periods;
- the process and accountable mailbox for privacy access, correction, deletion,
  complaint, and incident requests;
- Arabic and English versions convey the same commercial commitments; and
- the configured cancellation window displayed to customers matches the
  backend policy used at the time of cancellation.

Approval should include the policy version/effective date and the approving
person. Do not replace the policies with copied third-party templates: they
must remain consistent with NexRide's actual code and operations.
